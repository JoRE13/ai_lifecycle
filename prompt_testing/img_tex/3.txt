$|x-3| - |x+10| = 13$ fyrir x.

H
eighborsfum byr
eighbors tilviki.

(i) Ef $x \le -10$ 

p
eighbors er $-(x-3) - -(x+10) = 13 \Leftrightarrow -x+3+x+10 = 13 \Leftrightarrow 13=13$

(ii) Ef $-10 < x \le 3$ 

p
eighbors er $-(x-3) - (x+10) = 13 \Leftrightarrow -x+3-x-10 = 13 \Leftrightarrow -2x = 20 \Leftrightarrow x = -10$

(iii) Ef $x>3$

p
eighbors er $x-3 - (x+10) = 13 \Leftrightarrow x-3-x-10 = 13 \Leftrightarrow -13=13$

Svo $x \in ]-\infty, -10]$.
